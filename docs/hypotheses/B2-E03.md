# B2-E03 — PRE-REGISTRATION: why does L-BFGS solve the modal problem but fail on the full field?

**Written before any B2-E03 training.** This file is frozen once the first B2-E03 run starts. Later changes
are appended as dated amendments with reasons, and nothing above them is edited.

**Type:** causal mechanism-isolation experiment on the frozen full-field B1 benchmark. **No component is
novel:** L-BFGS, Adam, full-batch optimisation, collocation resampling and larger collocation sets are all
established. The purpose is to identify a bottleneck.

Spec: `configs/batch2/B2-E03_collocation_mechanism.yaml`. Code: `src/physref/collocation_lab.py`.
Evaluator: `scripts/analyze_b2_e03.py`, which implements §7–§10 exactly.

## 1. Research question

After restoring the spatial field, is the B2-E02 L-BFGS failure caused primarily by:
- **sparse / fixed collocation coverage**;
- **the quasi-Newton optimizer itself**; or
- **their interaction**?

**B2-E02 evidence, observational only** (`results_batch2/reports/B2-E02_REPORT.md` §4):

- **Modal, 1-D in t, 640 fixed points:** L-BFGS reaches the full window in 3/3 seeds.
- **Full field (x,t), the same 640 fixed points:**
  - L-BFGS is worse than B1 in 3/3 seeds;
  - the fixed-set residual reaches 0.003–0.005;
  - the dense-grid u-residual is 36–78× higher;
  - the collapse front stops at the switch.

## 2. Mathematical rationale (why coverage *could* matter, and why the optimizer *could*)

### Coverage

Use normalised coordinates ξ = x/L ∈ [0,1] and τ = t/P_d ∈ [0, 20.58], where P_d = 2π/ω_d is the damped
period. N uniform points cover an area of 20.58, so the typical spacing is h ≈ (20.58/N)^{1/2}:

| N | h | points per period along τ (and across the span) |
|---|---|---|
| 640 | 0.18 | ≈ 5.6 |
| 2,560 | 0.09 | ≈ 11 |

**Modal problem (1-D in τ).** 640 points give ≈ 31 points per period.

**Full-field strong residual.** It involves u_xxxx and u_tt, with a 241,601-parameter model and only 640
constraints. A full-batch optimizer can drive the residual to ~0 *at the points* (an under-determined fit)
while it stays large between them, especially for the 4th-order spatial operator.

**Mini-batch Adam (B1)** sees a fresh 640-point epoch every 5 steps, i.e. continual resampling. It cannot
interpolate one fixed set.

**Prediction if coverage dominates:**
- more points (4X) or fresh points (R) shrink the gap G = R_dense/R_train;
- dynamics improve;
- with the same fixed 640 points, Adam behaves like L-BFGS (it is also able to fit them).

### Optimizer

L-BFGS uses curvature pairs from a deterministic objective plus a strong-Wolfe line search with step
lr = 1. On a non-convex, ill-conditioned 4th-order landscape its large steps may move to a different
basin (for example a low-amplitude, over-damped solution) regardless of coverage.

**Prediction if the optimizer dominates:**
- full-batch Adam on the same fixed points does much better than L-BFGS;
- resampling or more points do not rescue L-BFGS.

## 3. Hypotheses (falsifiable; tested per seed, labels need the stated seed counts)

### Definitions (per seed; arm Y vs fixed-set L-BFGS F, all at the final model)

- **MB, materially better than F:** L2_Y ≤ 0.80·L2_F **and** R_dense,Y ≤ 0.80·R_dense,F **and**
  P_Y ≥ P_F − 0.5.
- **MW, materially worse than F:** L2_Y ≥ 1.25·L2_F **or** R_dense,Y ≥ 1.25·R_dense,F **or**
  P_Y ≤ P_F − 1.0.
- **RB1, restores B1-level performance:** L2_Y ≤ 1.10·L2_B1 **and** R_dense,Y ≤ 1.10·R_dense,B1 **and**
  P_Y ≥ P_B1 − 1.0 **and** |freq err_Y| ≤ 2 %.
- **IB1, improves over B1** (identical to the B2-E02 §10 transfer success): P_Y ≥ P_B1 + 3.98 **and**
  L2_Y ≤ 0.9·L2_B1 **and** R_dense,Y ≤ 1.1·R_dense,B1 **and** |freq err| ≤ 2 %.
- **OS, overfitting signature** (stage-2 arms; switch state S0 = `switch.pt` at 320k, S1 = final), all of:
  - R_train(S1) < R_train(S0)
  - R_dense(S1) > R_dense(S0)
  - G(S1) > G(S0)
  - P(S1) − P(S0) ≤ 0.5 cycles

### Hypotheses

**H1 (coverage).** The poor full-field L-BFGS transfer is caused substantially by optimisation against a
fixed, sparse collocation set.

| Verdict | Condition |
|---|---|
| **Supported** | F shows OS in ≥ 2/3 seeds **and** for at least one Y ∈ {LBFGS-R, LBFGS-4X}: MB in 3/3 seeds **and** G_Y ≤ 0.5·G_F in 3/3 seeds **and** OS absent for Y in ≥ 2/3 seeds |
| **Partially supported** | the same with "3/3" replaced by "2/3" |
| **Falsified** | no coverage arm is MB in any seed, **or** F does not show OS in ≥ 2/3 seeds (the phenomenon is not reproduced) |

**H2 (optimizer contributes).** Full-batch Adam on the same fixed 640 points does materially better than
L-BFGS.

| Verdict | Condition |
|---|---|
| **Supported** | ADAM-FULL is MB in 3/3 seeds |
| **Partially supported** | MB in exactly 2/3 seeds |
| **Falsified** | MB in 0/3 seeds |

**H3 (coverage dominant).**

| Verdict | Condition |
|---|---|
| **Supported** | LBFGS-R **and** LBFGS-4X both achieve RB1 in 3/3 seeds **and** F fails RB1 in 3/3 seeds **and** ADAM-FULL is not MB in ≥ 2/3 seeds |
| **Falsified** | either LBFGS-R or LBFGS-4X fails RB1 in ≥ 2/3 seeds |

**H4 (fixed-collocation explanation insufficient).**

| Verdict | Condition |
|---|---|
| **Supported** | none of LBFGS-R, LBFGS-4X, ADAM-FULL is MB in ≥ 2/3 seeds |

## 4. Arms (each seed: all arms start from bit-identical weights)

Seeds: **1234, 1235, 1236**.

### Stage 1, shared by every non-B1 arm

- The frozen Batch-1 `Trainer` loop, unchanged, for 2,500 steps × mini-batch 128 = **320,000 PDE
  evaluations**.
- It is bitwise identical to B1's first 2,500 steps (tested).
- The state at the switch is saved as `switch.pt`.

### Stage 2

**set0** = the next epoch draw (640 points) of the frozen sampler at the switch. It is identical in every
stage-2 arm (tested) and equals the B2-E02 transfer fixed set (asserted by the evaluator).

| Arm | Stage 2 | Collocation policy |
|---|---|---|
| **B1** (control) | none: 5,000 Adam steps, mini-batch 128, fresh 640-point epoch every 5 steps | sampler (unchanged) |
| **LBFGS-F** | torch L-BFGS: lr 1, history 50, strong-Wolfe, PyTorch default tolerances, float32 | set0 fixed throughout |
| **LBFGS-R** | same L-BFGS | **resample**: a fresh 640-point epoch draw every **50 closures** |
| **LBFGS-4X** | same L-BFGS | **reservoir**: 4 epoch draws (set0..set3 = 2,560 points) fixed at the switch; the active 640-point subset cycles set0 → set1 → set2 → set3 → set0 … every **50 closures** |
| **ADAM-FULL** | the **same Adam optimizer and lr schedule state** continued; **474 full-batch** steps on set0 (474 = the B2-E02 L-BFGS closure count, fixed a priori) | set0 fixed |

### Exact semantics

**Chunking.** All L-BFGS arms run in chunks of ≤ 50 closures: `max_eval` ≤ min(distance to the next 50-mark,
distance to the next snapshot, remaining − max_ls − 1). Set switches happen only at chunk ends, at the first
chunk end at or after each multiple of 50 closures. The exact closure index and PDE-evaluation count of every
switch are logged (`collocation.events`).

**History.** L-BFGS history is **not reset** when the active set changes. The only difference between F, R
and 4X is which points the closure evaluates.

**Why 50 closures.** One full L-BFGS history window per set. Declared a priori, not tuned.

**Expected number of stage-2 sets** (exact counts are logged):
- R: ≈ 10 sets (≈ 6,400 distinct points);
- 4X: 4 sets (2,560 distinct points), each revisited ≈ 2–3 times;
- F and ADAM-FULL: 1 set (640 points).

**Chunking relative to B2-E02.** B2-E02's fixed-set L-BFGS used chunks of up to 200 closures. Every new
`LBFGS.step` call begins with an extra closure evaluation, so chunking can change the trajectory.
LBFGS-F therefore uses the **same 50-closure chunking as R and 4X**, so that only the point set differs.

**Chunking sensitivity check.** If LBFGS-F achieves RB1 in ≥ 2/3 seeds, whereas B2-E02's fixed-set run
failed in 3/3, the CASE is still computed but **flagged "chunking-confounded"**, and the report must say so.

**Architecture.** No change in any arm: same network, hard IC/BC ansatz, physics, precision (float32),
sampler distribution and evaluator.

## 5. Compute budget and accounting

**Primary budget: 640,000 PDE evaluations per arm.**

| Event | PDE evaluations | Forward / backward passes |
|---|---|---|
| Stage-1 Adam step | 128 | 1 / 1 |
| Stage-2 closure (incl. **every line-search evaluation**) | 640 | 1 / 1 |
| ADAM-FULL step | 640 | 1 / 1 |
| Drawing or switching a point set | 0 | — |
| Snapshot evaluations | not training compute | — |

**Arm totals:**

| Arm | PDE evaluations |
|---|---|
| B1 | 640,000 |
| ADAM-FULL | exactly 320,000 + 474 × 640 = **623,360** |
| L-BFGS arms | ≤ 640,000, guaranteed by the max_eval guard; expected ≈ 623,360 |

**Compute STOP rule (fixed now).** STOP, and report without classification, if any stage-2 arm exceeds
640,000, **or** if the L-BFGS arms' PDE evaluations differ from ADAM-FULL's 623,360 by more than **3 %**
(19,200 evaluations).

**Also recorded:**
- optimizer steps (stage-1 Adam steps);
- stage-2 closures / full-batch steps;
- L-BFGS iterations;
- forward and backward passes;
- wall-clock (`train_seconds`, training work only);
- peak RAM;
- parameters (241,601);
- model size.

**Primary comparison:** fixed PDE budget. Wall-clock is reported separately.

**Execution.** One batch per seed: all 5 arms concurrently, one process and one thread each, on the 4-vCPU
machine. The oversubscription (5 processes on 4 vCPU) is declared and is identical for every arm in a batch.
Wall-clock is compared only within a batch.

## 6. Initialisation policy

For every seed, every arm builds the frozen B1 model with that seed. The sha256 over all state-dict tensors
is written to `<run>/init_checksum.json` (B1 included).

**STOP** if the checksums of the 5 arms differ within a seed. This is asserted by the evaluator and was also
tested in `tests/test_b2_collocation_lab.py`.

## 7. Snapshots and evaluation (identical for every arm)

**Snapshots:**
- stage 1 every 1,000 steps (128,000 evaluations), as established;
- `switch.pt` at 320,000 (stage-2 arms);
- in stage 2, the first state at or after each 128,000 multiple;
- the final model.

Snapshot cadence is metadata only; the switch snapshot is identical for all stage-2 arms.

**At every evaluated point, with the frozen B2-E01 evaluator:**
- L2_exact and L2_paper (201×2001);
- persistence (frozen Batch-1 definition), velocity persistence, collapse time t_c and cycles;
- fitted ω (frequency error) and fitted decay;
- amplitude ratio and max|u_t| ratio;
- cumulative PDE evaluations and wall-clock.

**Residual definitions.** r = c²u_xxxx + u_tt + γu_t of the **physical displacement u**: the strong residual,
Batch-1 `pde_residual`, reverse-mode autograd.

    R_dense = RMS_{grid}(r) / RMS_{grid}(u_tt,exact)          51 × 501 uniform grid incl. boundaries
                                                              (= Batch-1 physics_metrics PDE_residual_rel)
    R_train = RMS_{S}(r)    / RMS_{grid}(u_tt,exact)          S = the training set at that snapshot (same denominator)
    G       = R_dense / R_train          ΔR = R_dense − R_train

**Training set S at a snapshot:**

| Situation | S |
|---|---|
| Stage 1 and B1 | the sampler's current 640-point epoch set (stored in the checkpoint) |
| LBFGS-F, ADAM-FULL | set0 |
| LBFGS-R | the **active** set |
| LBFGS-4X | **primary**: the whole 2,560-point reservoir. Also reported: the active subset |

**At `switch.pt`**, R_train is evaluated on the **stage-2** training set of that arm (set0; the reservoir for
LBFGS-4X; set0 for LBFGS-R, its first active set). The OS test (§3) therefore compares R_train on the same
point set before and after stage 2. The sets are read from the arm's final checkpoint.

**Independence of the dense grid.** The dense grid is a deterministic `linspace` grid; training points are
float64 uniform draws. The evaluator asserts that **no training point coincides with a grid node**
(|Δx| > 1e-12 or |Δt| > 1e-12). **STOP** if this is violated.

The **IC and BC errors** of the final model are reported.

## 8. Decision matrix (computed in code; CASE labels are mutually exclusive)

Let:
- Cov3 = (LBFGS-R **or** LBFGS-4X is MB in 3/3 seeds);
- Cov2 = (one of them is MB in ≥ 2/3 seeds);
- Opt3 = (ADAM-FULL is MB in 3/3 seeds);
- Opt2 = (ADAM-FULL is MB in ≥ 2/3 seeds).

| CASE | Condition | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Collocation coverage dominates** | Cov3 **and not** Opt2 | — |
| **B — Optimizer dominates** | Opt3 **and not** Cov2 | — |
| **C — Interaction** | Cov2 **and** Opt2 | both coverage and the optimizer change performance materially |
| **D — Hypothesis rejected / not attributable** | otherwise | includes single-factor results in only 2/3 seeds, and "all similar" |

Also reported, but not used to assign the CASE:
- RB1 / IB1 per arm and seed: does anything *fully* solve the problem?
- OS per arm and seed;
- the H1–H4 verdicts (§3);
- the chunking-sensitivity flag (§4).

## 9. Memory Pareto rule

**Memory is a Pareto metric.** Per seed, the 5 arms are compared on:
- (L2_exact ↓, train_seconds ↓, peak_rss_mb ↓);
- (persistence ↑, train_seconds ↓, peak_rss_mb ↓).

**Memory-aware dominance:** X dominates Y if L2_X ≤ L2_Y, P_X ≥ P_Y, W_X ≤ W_Y and RAM_X ≤ RAM_Y, with at
least one strict.

The non-dominated set per seed is reported. **Any arm later proposed as a component must be non-dominated in
≥ 2/3 seeds with memory included.**

## 10. Transfer-interpretation rules

| Outcome | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| CASE A, and a coverage arm achieves **IB1** in 3/3 seeds | "The modal L-BFGS benefit survives restoring the spatial field **when collocation coverage is adequate**." Still not a novelty claim; it requires a separately pre-registered confirmation |
| CASE A, coverage arms achieve **RB1 but not IB1** | "Coverage removes the L-BFGS failure but adds nothing over B1 at this budget." |
| CASE B | "Quasi-Newton optimisation on this full-field landscape is itself harmful, independently of coverage." The modal success is then a property of the reduced 1-D landscape |
| CASE C | both mechanisms matter; the strategy must control **both** coverage and optimizer |
| CASE D | the fixed-collocation explanation is insufficient (H4); another mechanism must be investigated before any controller design |

## 11. STOP conditions (PI)

1. Unequal compute (§5 rule).
2. Initialisation differs (§6).
3. The dense grid is contaminated (§7).
4. The frozen B1 control changes. B2-E03-B1 must reproduce the Phase-A B1 run of the same seed
   **bit-exactly** (asserted).
5. Undisclosed extra L-BFGS budget.
6. A result depends on a post-hoc threshold.
7. An undeclared architectural change.

A falsified hypothesis is reported as falsified.

## Amendment 1 — 2026-10-07 ~23:25 UTC (appended; nothing above edited)

**Trigger.** While seed 1234 was running, `B2-E03-LBFGS-4X-s1234` reached a **non-finite loss** at
stage-2 closure 401 (576,640 PDE evaluations). This happened in active subset set3, on its second visit.
All parameters became non-finite and the run ended with status `diverged`. There is no final model. Its last
finite snapshot is at 512,000 evaluations.

This amendment was written **before** any metric of any other B2-E03 arm or seed was looked at. The other four
seed-1234 runs were still training.

**Gap in the pre-registration.** The pre-registration did not state how a diverged run is classified. The
§5 compute rule ("L-BFGS arms within 3 % of 623,360") was written to catch *accounting* inequality. A
divergence is not one: the accounting rules were identical, and the run stopped because the loss became
non-finite.

**Rule added. It introduces no threshold and is the definitional consequence of having no finite final
model:**

1. **Classification of a diverged run.** It **cannot satisfy** MB, RB1 or IB1 (each needs a finite final
   L2, R_dense and P). It counts as **MW** (materially worse than LBFGS-F). OS is "not evaluable" and counts
   as absent for H1. It is classified with the same CASE logic (§8): MB = false in that seed.
2. **Compute.** A diverged run is exempt from the 3 % equality check. Its evaluations at divergence are
   reported, and it still must not exceed 640,000. The 3 % rule applies unchanged to every completed run.
3. **Reporting.** All finite snapshots of a diverged run are evaluated with the identical evaluator and
   reported descriptively (trajectory up to divergence). The divergence point (closure index, PDE
   evaluations, active set) is reported.
4. **No re-run, no re-tuning.** The diverged run is not repeated, and no setting (switch_every, line search,
   history reset, precision) is changed.
