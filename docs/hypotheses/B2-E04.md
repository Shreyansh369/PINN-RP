**Type: post-hoc mechanistic analysis of EXISTING checkpoints. NO TRAINING.**

**Status:** written and committed **before** any diagnostic value of any trained checkpoint was computed.

  - a timing/memory probe on the *reconstructed initial state* of seed 1234 (no diagnostic value was computed or printed; `§11`);
  - the identity checks of `§3.3`.

  script is copied from this document.

## 0. Rules for this phase

**Prohibited:**
- Training of any kind: no Adam, L-BFGS, causal weighting, adaptive sampling, new architectures or new seeds.
- New training checkpoints.
- Changes to B2-E01/E02/E03 training code, configurations, checkpoints, reports or results.

**How checkpoints are used:**
- They are only `torch.load`-ed.
- Their `model` state is copied into a freshly built module of the frozen architecture.
- Diagnostics are evaluated on a float64 copy of that module.
- No optimizer is constructed and no parameter is updated.

  The exception is the dry-run finite-difference check (`§11`). It perturbs the in-memory float64 copy by ±ε and
  restores it, and nothing is ever written back.

**Outputs:** only to the new paths `results_batch2/B2-E04/`, `results_batch2/B2-E04_*.{csv,json}`,
`results_batch2/figures/B2-E04/` and `results_batch2/reports/B2-E04_REPORT.md`. Writes are guarded by
`physref.safety.assert_safe_output(strict=True)`, and existing files are never overwritten.

**Scientific position:**
- Spectral bias, gradient conflict, temporal causality and operator/Jacobian conditioning are **established
  research concepts** (`§12`). B2-E04 does not claim to discover any of them.
- It asks a narrower question: which **measurable** quantity is associated with the observed failure on **this**
  beam benchmark, whether it changes **before** the dynamic degradation, and whether it differs between the modal
  and full-field problems.

## 1. Evidence carried forward (B2-E01–E03, reports in `results_batch2/reports/`)

1. FP64 does not materially change the trajectory (B2-E01, 3/3 seeds).
2. Mixed formulation (derivative order 4 → 2) is cheaper but never more accurate (B2-E01).
3. The modal problem (exact φ₁ supplied, q(t) learned): Adam (M0) collapses at 0.30–0.37 s, and its front
   retreats after 384–512k evaluations. Adam→L-BFGS (switch at 320k, fixed 640-point set) reaches the full
   window in 3/3 seeds (B2-E02).
4. The same Adam→L-BFGS on the full field is worse than B1 in 3/3 seeds (B2-E02T).
5. A 2,560-point reservoir nearly removes the train/dense residual gap but does not restore propagation.
   Full-batch Adam on the fixed set stalls. Resampled L-BFGS helps inconsistently (B2-E03, CASE D).
6. Hence sparse-collocation overfitting is not sufficient to explain the failure.

**Known physical facts used in the design** (from the reports, not from any B2-E04 diagnostic):
- Full-field collapse fronts lie at t_c ≈ 0.03–0.25 s at every checkpoint, i.e. **inside W₁ = [0, 0.25]**.
- Modal M0 fronts lie at 0.15–0.37 s.
- Modal L-BFGS reaches t_c = 1 s (no collapse) by 512k–623k.

This is why front-relative windows (`§5.3`) are pre-registered **in addition to** the mandated W₁–W₄.

## 2. Authoritative implementation (what the repository computes; nothing reconstructed from memory)

**Benchmark: FE-D-M1** (`beampinn.physics.benchmarks`):
- fixed–fixed beam, L = 2.75 m, T = 1 s, A0 = 0.08 m;
- training PDE coefficients `paper_eq49`: c2 = 43.73² = 1912.3129 (coefficient of u_xxxx) and γ = 7.08
  (coefficient of u_t);
- exact eigen-root β₁L = 4.730041 (`eigen_root`), giving ω₁ = β₁²√c2 = 129.37 rad/s, ω_d = √(ω₁² − γ²/4)
  and P_d = 2π/ω_d = 0.04859 s.

**Physical residual** (`beampinn.losses.residuals.pde_residual`, `pde_scale` = 1):
`r = c2·u_xxxx + u_tt + γ·u_t`.

In the task statement's notation, α ≡ c2 and c ≡ γ. The diagnostic split used in `§5.4` is:
- r_time = u_tt + γ·u_t;
- r_space = c2·u_xxxx;
- r = r_time + r_space.

The split is asserted equal to the frozen `pde_residual` in the dry-run.

**Full-field model** (`HardConstrainedFF`, frozen B1):
- u = u0(x) + tanh²(ω₁t)·Φ(x)·A0·N_θ(x,t), with Φ = 16x²(L−x)²/L⁴ and u0 = A0·φ₁(x);
- N_θ = the spatio-temporal Fourier network: 6×200 tanh, m = 100, σ_x = (1), σ_t = (10, 1), standardised inputs;
- 241,601 trainable parameters;
- training loss = ½·mean(r²) over mini-batches of 128 drawn from 640-point epochs.

**Modal model** (`physref.formulations.modal`, B2-E01/E02):
- q(t) = 1 + tanh²(ω₁t)·N_θ(t), with N_θ the B1 temporal branch (identical initial weights);
- u = A0·φ₁(x)·q(t) (`ModalField`);
- training residual = A0·(q'' + γq' + ω₁²q), with ω₁² = c2·β₁⁴ (`ArmTrainer.loss`);
- 241,601 trainable parameters.

For this problem the diagnostic split is r_time = A0(q'' + γq') and r_space = A0·ω₁²·q. This is the Galerkin
projection of (u_tt + γu_t) and c2·u_xxxx onto φ₁: for u = A0φ₁q, c2·u_xxxx = A0·ω₁²·φ₁·q exactly.

**Mixed model** (B2-E01): (u, v = u_xx) with a two-output head. Its training residuals (r_link, r_pde) differ from
the strong residual. It is therefore used **only** in Diagnostics A and B, on its displacement u; see `§3.1`.

**Spatial eigenfunctions** φ_n(x), n = 1..8:
- built from the exact fixed–fixed roots of cos z·cosh z = 1, `mode_shape_raw(β_n)`;
- normalised to max|φ_n| = 1, sign +1 at argmax |U_n|;
- φ₁ is asserted identical to the benchmark mode shape (`BeamCase.mode_shape`);
- with exact roots they are L²-orthogonal (asserted under the quadrature of `§4`).

The exact reference is u_ex = A0·φ₁(x)·q_ex(t), with q_ex = `modal_time(ω₁, γ, t)`, used **only for evaluation**:
- the a₁ error;
- energy normalisation;
- the frozen L2 and persistence metrics.

It never enters a model.

**Frozen evaluation:**
- L2_exact / L2_paper on the 201×2001 grid (`l2_pair`);
- persistence / collapse time via `physref.persistence`: local amplitude ratio < 0.5 over a one-period
  window at mid-span, N_TRACE = 4001.

**Checkpoint format.** `torch.save` dicts with keys `model` (state_dict), `config`, `acc` (incl.
`pde_evaluations`), `status`, `step`.

**Collocation storage.**
- B1 points are not stored; only the sampler state is.
- The E02/E03 fixed sets are stored in the blobs.

B2-E04 does not use training points. All diagnostic point sets are new and deterministic (`§4`, `§5`, `§6`).

## 4. Diagnostic A — modal decomposition of the predicted field

### 4.1 Definitions

**Grid A:**
- x: 64 Gauss–Legendre nodes on [0, L] with weights w_i;
- t: 4001 uniform nodes on [0, T], the frozen persistence-trace grid.

**Number of modes:** N = 8 (fixed). The benchmark does not mathematically require another number.
Content beyond mode 8 is captured by the remainder.

**Mode coefficients:** for each t,

  a_n(t) = Σ_i w_i u(x_i,t) φ_n(x_i) / m_n,   with m_n = Σ_i w_i φ_n(x_i)² = ‖φ_n‖².

**Energies:**
- mode energy e_n(t) = m_n·a_n(t)²;
- field energy e_u(t) = Σ_i w_i u²;
- remainder e_rem(t) = Σ_i w_i (u − Σ_n a_n φ_n)²;
- exact first-mode energy e_ex(t) = m₁·(A0 q_ex(t))².

The user's E_n(t) = |a_n|²/Σ|a_k|² is reported in the energy-correct form f_n = e_n / Σ_{k≤8} e_k, because the
φ_n norms differ.

### 4.2 Metrics per region R (`§4.3`)

Means are taken over the t-nodes in R.

- **Energy relative to the exact signal:**
  - ρ_n = ⟨e_n⟩_R / ⟨e_ex⟩_R;
  - ρ_hi = Σ_{n=2..8} ρ_n;
  - ρ_2–4 and ρ_5–8 (the two halves of ρ_hi);
  - ρ_even (antisymmetric modes 2, 4, 6, 8: spurious for a symmetric problem);
  - ρ_rem;
  - **ρ_nonfund = ρ_hi + ρ_rem**;
  - ρ_tot = ⟨e_u⟩/⟨e_ex⟩.
- **Energy fractions:** f_n and f_hi = Σ_{n≥2} f_n.
- **First-mode amplitude error:** ε₁ = ‖a₁ − A0 q_ex‖_R / ‖A0 q_ex‖_R.
- **First-mode collapse time t_c1:** the frozen collapse rule (first t ≥ P_d/2 with local-amplitude ratio < 0.5,
  one-period window) applied to a₁(t) instead of the mid-span displacement.
- **Non-fundamental onset time t_h:** first t ≥ P_d/2 at which the one-period moving average of
  Σ_{n≥2} e_n + e_rem reaches **0.05** × the moving average of e_ex. NaN if never.
- **Post-front character** (only if t_c < T − P_d). Let missing = max(0, 1 − ρ₁(POST)). Then:
  - **REDISTRIBUTION** if ρ_nonfund(POST) ≥ 0.5·missing;
  - **AMPLITUDE_LOSS** if ρ_nonfund(POST) ≤ 0.1·missing;
  - **MIXED** otherwise;
  - **NOT_COLLAPSED** if t_c ≥ T − P_d.

### 4.3 Regions

- **W₁** = [0, 0.25], **W₂** = (0.25, 0.5], **W₃** = (0.5, 0.75], **W₄** = (0.75, 1];
- **FULL** = [0, T];
- **PRE** = [0, t_c), behind the front: the region still reproduced;
- **POST** = [t_c, T], ahead of the front. Only if t_c < T − P_d.

t_c is the frozen collapse time of that checkpoint, computed by the frozen metric before any diagnostic.

### 4.4 Questions answered (numerically)

1. Does a₁ vanish beyond the front? Answered by ρ₁(POST) and t_c1 vs t_c.
2. Does energy migrate into higher modes? Answered by ρ_hi(POST) and t_h.
3. Is total modal content preserved while a₁ decays? Answered by ρ_tot(POST) vs ρ₁(POST).
4. Is there spurious high-frequency spatial content? Answered by ρ_5–8, ρ_rem and ρ_even, both PRE and POST.
5. Amplitude loss or redistribution? Answered by the post-front character.

For MO, all ρ_{n≥2} and ρ_rem are 0 **by construction**. This is a structural fact and is reported as such.

## 5. Diagnostics B, C, D (residual spectrum; temporal-window gradients; space/time operator gradients)

### 5.1 Diagnostic B — residual spectrum

**Grid B:** x = the same 64 Gauss–Legendre nodes; t = 501 uniform nodes (frozen PDE_NT).

**Residual:** r(x,t), together with r_time and r_space, from the authoritative autograd derivatives.
- For MO, the field is the `ModalField` view, so this is the physical residual of the modal field.
- For MX, it is the strong residual of its u.

**Projection:**
- r_n(t) = Σ_i w_i r φ_n / m_n;
- s_n = m_n·r_n²;
- s_r = Σ_i w_i r²;
- s_rem = Σ_i w_i (r − Σ r_n φ_n)².

**Metrics per region R** (`§4.3`, on the 501-node grid):
- residual energy fraction by mode, σ_n = ⟨s_n⟩/⟨s_r⟩, plus σ_rem and σ_even;
- **h_r = 1 − σ₁**: the non-fundamental share, i.e. high-mode concentration including the remainder;
- **dominant mode** = argmax over {1..8, rem};
- **spectral centroid** κ_r = Σ n⟨s_n⟩ / Σ⟨s_n⟩, over n ≤ 8;
- R_norm = √(⟨s_r⟩/L) / RMS(u_tt,exact), the same normalisation as B2-E03;
- mode-1 fractions of r_time and of r_space separately.

For MO, σ₁ = 1 by construction (structural check).

### 5.2 Set C (gradient diagnostics C and D)

**Points:**
- t: 256 midpoints t_j = (j + ½)/256 s;
- FF only: x = 16 Gauss–Legendre nodes.

**Weights:** ω = the Gauss–Legendre x-weight for FF; 1 for MO.

**Chunks:** 32 chunks of 8 consecutive t-midpoints each, covering [c/32, (c+1)/32] s.

**Per chunk, accumulated in float64** (sg = stop-gradient):
- G_T = ∇_θ Σ ω·sg(r)·r_time;
- G_S = ∇_θ Σ ω·sg(r)·r_space;
- H_T = ∇_θ Σ ω·sg(r_time)·r_time;
- H_S = ∇_θ Σ ω·sg(r_space)·r_space;
- Ω = Σ ω.

**For any union S of chunks:**
- g_S = Σ(G_T + G_S)/Ω. This is ∇_θ of the quadrature population loss ½⟨r²⟩_S, verified in the dry-run against
  direct autograd and by finite differences.
- g_time = 2ΣH_T/Ω = ∇⟨r_time²⟩_S.
- g_space = 2ΣH_S/Ω = ∇⟨r_space²⟩_S.
- g_T = ΣG_T/Ω and g_S = ΣG_S/Ω, so that g_T + g_S = g.

For MO, r = A0(q'' + γq' + ω₁²q) is the modal training residual (`§2`).

**Population loss, not the stochastic gradient.** These are gradients of the deterministic quadrature population
loss (the uniform measure the trainers sample from), not the stochastic mini-batch gradient.

### 5.3 Diagnostic C — temporal-window gradient conflict

**Primary, the mandated windows.** W₁..W₄ = chunks 0–7, 8–15, 16–23 and 24–31. For each window,
g_i = ∇_θ L_{W_i}, and the following are reported:
- the full 4×4 cosine matrix C_ij;
- **min C** = min_{i<j} C_ij;
- **frac_neg** = the fraction of the 6 pairs with C_ij < 0;
- ‖g_i‖;
- **starvation** = ‖g₄‖/‖g₁‖;
- max_{i≥2}‖g_i‖/‖g₁‖;
- cos(g_i, g_FULL).

**Secondary, front-relative windows** (pre-registered here because every full-field front lies inside W₁, `§1`).
Windows are defined on the chunk grid:
- **BEHIND** = chunks with upper edge ≤ t_c − P_d;
- **AHEAD** = chunks with lower edge ≥ t_c + P_d;
- **FRONT** = the rest;
- all undefined if t_c ≥ T − P_d, and an empty set gives NaN.

Reported:
- cos(g_BEHIND, g_FRONT), cos(g_FRONT, g_AHEAD) and cos(g_BEHIND, g_AHEAD);
- ‖g_FRONT‖/‖g_BEHIND‖ and ‖g_AHEAD‖/‖g_FRONT‖.

### 5.4 Diagnostic D — space/time operator gradients

These are **diagnostic** decompositions. No trainer optimized separate temporal and spatial losses, and nothing
here alters any training objective.

**Over FULL** (all 32 chunks), each of W₁..W₄ and BEHIND:
- L_time = ⟨r_time²⟩ and L_space = ⟨r_space²⟩;
- **C_st** = cos(g_time, g_space);
- ‖g_time‖ and ‖g_space‖;
- **ratio** = ‖g_space‖/‖g_time‖.

**Secondary, D2** (decomposition of the *actual* training gradient):
- **C_TS** = cos(g_T, g_S);
- the cancellation index **χ** = ‖g_T + g_S‖/(‖g_T‖ + ‖g_S‖) ∈ [0, 1]. χ → 0 means the training gradient is a
  small difference of large, opposing temporal- and spatial-operator contributions.

## 6. Diagnostic E — residual-Jacobian spectral conditioning

**What is estimated.** J = ∂r/∂θ ∈ ℝ^{N×P}, with N = **640** points and P = 241,601.
- **Set J:** x = U(0, L), t = U(0, T), drawn by `torch.Generator().manual_seed(20261008)` in float64 (x first, then
  t).
- **Independence:** this set is fresh and independent of every training set.
- **Size:** 640 is the size of one training epoch and of one L-BFGS closure set.
- **Residual per problem:** FF uses the strong residual at (x_p, t_p). MO uses its training residual
  A0(q'' + γq' + ω₁²q) at t_p.
- **Comparability:** each problem's Jacobian is that of its own training residual. This is the Gauss–Newton factor of
  its loss, JᵀJ/N.

**Estimator.**
- **Rows:** each of the 640 rows is computed **exactly** by reverse mode in float64, in chunks of 32 points.
- **Singular values:** obtained by a Householder QR of Jᵀ (LAPACK `dgeqrf`, in place) followed by the SVD of the
  640×640 triangular factor. This is backward stable, with absolute error ~ ε‖J‖.
- **Memory:** J is held once (1.24 GB float64). No N×N Gram matrix is formed (its squaring would halve the
  resolvable range), and no P×P matrix is formed.
- **The only approximation is the finite point sample.** The result is therefore called the **"estimated residual-Jacobian
  spectral condition"**. It is the exact spectral condition of the 640-point sampled residual Jacobian. It is not
  the condition number of the continuous operator, and not that of the loss Hessian (the Hessian adds the
  Σ r·∇²r term).

**Numerical tolerances** (verified in the dry-run, `§11`):
- resolution threshold **τ_res = 1e-10**, with ε₆₄·√N = 5.6e-15 ≪ τ_res;
- rows computed with chunk 32 vs 16 agree to < 1e-12 relative;
- QR-route σ agree with `numpy.linalg.svd` within 1e-6 relative for σ ≥ τ_res·σ₁ (128-row test);
- Jᵀr/N equals ∇½⟨r²⟩ to 1e-10.

**Metrics:**
- σ₁ and σ_N;
- numerical rank r(τ) = #{σ_i ≥ τσ₁}, for τ ∈ {1e-2, 1e-4, 1e-6, 1e-8, 1e-10};
- **σ_res** = the smallest σ_i ≥ τ_res·σ₁, the "smallest resolvable singular value";
- **κ̂ = σ₁/σ_res**, reported as log10 κ̂;
- the flag **saturated** = r(τ_res) < N. When saturated, κ̂ ≈ 1/τ_res by construction, and the informative quantity
  is the rank r(τ);
- spectral decay σ_k/σ₁ for k ∈ {2, 5, 10, 20, 50, 100, 200, 400, 640};
- entropy effective rank exp(−Σ p_i ln p_i), with p_i = σ_i/Σσ;
- secondary: per-window spectra (rows with t ∈ W_i, about 160 each), with σ₁, r(1e-6) and log10 κ̂.

**Primary conditioning summaries:** log10 κ̂ and r(1e-6).

## 7. Pathology criteria (per state; thresholds fixed here)

| Mechanism | Criterion (state is "pathological" if ANY holds) | Physical-time location |
|---|---|---|
| **A** spectral / modal | ρ_nonfund(PRE) ≥ **0.05**; or h_r(PRE) ≥ **0.5**; or post-front character ∈ {REDISTRIBUTION, MIXED} | the first two are **behind the front**; the third is ahead |
| **B** temporal gradient | min C(W₁..W₄) ≤ **−0.1**; or cos(g_BEHIND, g_FRONT) ≤ **−0.1**; or cos(g_FRONT, g_AHEAD) ≤ **−0.1**; or starvation ‖g₄‖/‖g₁‖ ≤ **0.01** | behind–front = **before**; front–ahead = **at**; window-level / starvation = undetermined |
| **C** space/time | C_st(FULL) ≤ **−0.5**; or ratio ≥ **10** or ≤ **0.1** | **before**, if the same criterion holds on BEHIND |
| **D** conditioning | deterioration relative to the pre-switch reference state (FF: 320k; MO: 256k, since 320k is unavailable): Δlog10 κ̂ ≥ **1** (≥ 10×), or r(1e-6) ≤ **0.75** × reference | training-time only |

NaN values (e.g. front windows of an uncollapsed modal L-BFGS state) count as **not pathological**.

## 8. Paired comparisons and margins

Paired by seed. K_post = {384k, 512k, end}.
- **Δ_full** = D(FF-LBFGS) − D(FF-B1). This is failing vs continuing, from the **same** 320k parent state.
- **Δ_modal** = D(MO-LBFGS) − D(MO-ADAM). This is succeeding vs continuing, from the same 256k state (the switch
  occurred at 320k).

**Primary scalars** (direction that counts as pathological, with margin):

| Mechanism | Primary scalars |
|---|---|
| A | h_r(PRE) ↑ (margin 0.1); ρ_nonfund(PRE) ↑ (margin 0.02) |
| B | min C ↓ (0.1); cos(g_BEHIND, g_FRONT) ↓ (0.1) |
| C | C_st ↓ (0.1); \|log10 ratio\| ↑ (0.3 decades) |
| D | log10 κ̂ ↑ (0.3 decades); r(1e-6) ↓ (10 % of the comparator) |

A paired difference is "pathological" if **any** primary scalar of the mechanism moves in its pathological
direction by more than its margin. The margins are effect-size floors, fixed here. With three seeds no
significance test is meaningful; consistency across seeds is required instead (`§9`).

**Adam contrast (descriptive):** B1 vs M0 at 0–640k. It describes what differs between the full field and the modal
problem under identical Adam training. It does not enter the CASE logic.

## 9. Temporal ordering and decision framework

### 9.1 Degradation events (from the frozen persistence metric only)

- **Transfer fall-behind:** the first level k ∈ K_post with t_c(FF-LBFGS, k) < t_c(FF-B1, k) − P_d/2.
- **M0 retreat:** the first level at which t_c falls below its running maximum by more than P_d/2.

### 9.2 Ordering

- **O_train** (per seed). Find the first level along FF-LBFGS (0 → 623k, shared states included) at which the
  mechanism's criterion holds, and compare it with the fall-behind event: **precedes**, **coincides**, **follows**,
  **absent** or no_event.
  - The shared states (≤ 320k) are identical for B1 and the transfer. A criterion that already holds there
    therefore "precedes" but does not by itself discriminate; discrimination is required separately by S.
- **O_phys** (per seed). The physical-time location (`§7`) of the criterion at the event checkpoint:
  - **before** = behind the front;
  - **at** = at the front;
  - none.
- **O** holds in a seed if O_train ∈ {precedes, coincides} or O_phys ∈ {before, at}.

  A quantity that is pathological **only** ahead of the front **and** only after the event is "after collapse".
  It cannot be the primary mechanism.

### 9.3 Per-mechanism tests (each evaluated per seed)

- **R (reproducible):** the criterion holds for FF-LBFGS at ≥ 2 of the 3 K_post levels.
- **S1 (spatial coupling):** R holds, **and** the criterion is false for MO-LBFGS at ≥ 2 of the 3 K_post levels.
  - Not applicable to A: the modal field has no non-fundamental content by construction, so S1 would hold
    trivially.
- **S2 (optimization):** Δ_full is pathological at ≥ 2 K_post levels, **and** Δ_modal is not pathological at ≥ 2
  K_post levels.
  - For A, only the Δ_full part is required (Δ_modal ≡ 0 structurally).

### 9.4 Verdict per mechanism (seed counts over 3 seeds)

- **R**, **S** and **O** each hold if they hold in ≥ 2 of 3 seeds.
- S = S1 ∨ S2. For A, S = S2.
- **SUPPORTED** ⇔ R ∧ S ∧ O.
- **PARTIAL** ⇔ R ∧ (S xor O).
- **NOT_SUPPORTED** otherwise.

### 9.5 CASE (mandated letters)

- **A**: spectral/modal mechanism.
- **B**: temporal gradient conflict.
- **C**: space/time gradient conflict.
- **D**: conditioning.
- **E**: combined mechanism, if ≥ 2 mechanisms are SUPPORTED. Each must satisfy O independently, which is built
  into SUPPORTED.
- **F**: no clear mechanism, if none is SUPPORTED. PARTIAL results are reported as such and **do not** make a
  CASE.

### 9.6 Answers that are always reported, regardless of the CASE

The step-9 table (Modal | Full field | Evidence) is filled from the measured criteria only. It covers:
- the **spatial coupling test**: which diagnostic changes between MO and FF at equal levels;
- the **temporal propagation test**: which criteria hold for MO-ADAM around its retreat;
- the **optimization test**: Δ_full vs Δ_modal after the switch.

**Descriptive statistics:**
- per seed: value, mean, std (ddof = 1) and range; individual seeds are always shown;
- the Spearman association between each primary scalar at level k and the front advance t_c(k+1) − t_c(k),
  per trajectory;
- descriptive comparisons of κ̂ with L2, persistence, modal energy and gradient conflict.

**Interpretation rules:**
- Association is never called causation.
- A SUPPORTED mechanism means: "measurable on this benchmark, reproducible, discriminating, and not
  only after collapse".

## 10. What would falsify each mechanism (explicit)

- **A is falsified** if, for the failing full-field trajectories:
  - the post-front character is AMPLITUDE_LOSS in ≥ 2/3 seeds at the end level, i.e. a₁ → 0 while higher modes
    do not grow;
  - **and** ρ_nonfund(PRE) < 0.05 and h_r(PRE) < 0.5 at all K_post levels.
- **B is falsified** if no window pair conflicts (min C > −0.1), front-relative cosines are > −0.1, and starvation
  > 0.01, in ≥ 2/3 seeds. It is also falsified if conflict/starvation appears **only** ahead of the front.
- **C is falsified** if C_st > −0.5 and 0.1 < ratio < 10 for FF-LBFGS in ≥ 2/3 seeds. It is also falsified if
  these values do not differ from the modal values beyond the margins.
- **D is falsified** if κ̂ and r(1e-6) do not deteriorate after the switch (Δlog10 κ̂ < 1 and rank ≥ 75 %) in
  ≥ 2/3 seeds. It is also falsified if the deterioration is equal for the successful modal L-BFGS.

## 11. Computational discipline (dry-run, then full run)

**Cost probe (before this document, init state of s1234, timing only, float64, 1 thread):**
- forward + backward of the strong residual: 1.2 s per 640 points;
- per-row Jacobian: about 5.5 s per 64 rows, i.e. about 1 min per 640 rows;
- SVD of a 640 × 241,601 matrix: 27 s.

**Estimated runtime:**
- FF state: about 3–5 min. MO states are much cheaper (no x-derivatives).
- 75 states with 3 single-thread processes (about 4 GB each): about 1–1.5 h.

**Dry-run.** The pre-registered checkpoint is **`FF_b1-128k_s1234`** (`B2-E01-B1-s1234` step_1000). It is chosen
by position (the first trained checkpoint), not by value. Checks:
- (i) basis orthogonality < 1e-10; the exact solution projects to a₁ = A0·q_ex (< 1e-10) with
  non-fundamental energy < 1e-18;
- (ii) the residual split equals the frozen `pde_residual` (< 1e-12), and the exact reference residual is ~0
  (< 1e-9 relative);
- (iii) a central finite-difference directional derivative of the W₁ loss agrees with g₁·v (< 1e-5), and g₁
  equals direct autograd of the window loss (< 1e-10);
- (iv) Jacobian chunk invariance, Jᵀr/N identity, and QR-vs-SVD agreement (`§6`);
- (v) runtime and memory of one full unit;
- (vi) the checkpoint sha256 is unchanged; outputs go only to `results_batch2/B2-E04/dryrun/`.

**If a check fails:**
- A code bug may be fixed. The fix is documented in an amendment and does not change any definition or threshold.
- A methodology change is not allowed. If the Jacobian computation proves infeasible, the analysis **STOPS** and the
  limitation is reported; no estimator is silently substituted.

**Full run** (after the dry-run is clean):
- `manifest before` (with integrity);
- `chmod a-w` on the source checkpoints;
- `run --workers 3`;
- `manifest after`;
- `aggregate`, followed by the report.

## 12. Literature and novelty limitations (from `docs/LITERATURE_MATRIX.md`, plus a targeted check)

Every diagnostic family used here is established.
- **Spectral bias / modal behaviour:**
  - NTK eigen-spectrum and convergence-rate disparity (Wang, Yu & Perdikaris, JCP 2022, arXiv:2007.14527; matrix
    B1);
  - multiscale Fourier features and eigenvector bias (Wang, Wang & Perdikaris 2021, F2);
  - Fourier features (Tancik et al. 2020, F1);
  - modal / reduced-order PINNs (F9, F10, H8).
- **Gradient conflict:**
  - learning-rate annealing and gradient pathologies (Wang, Teng & Perdikaris 2021, A8);
  - ConFIG (Liu, Chu & Thuerey, ICLR 2025, B5; arXiv:2408.11104);
  - gradient alignment and SOAP (Wang et al., NeurIPS 2025, A4; arXiv:2502.00604).
- **Temporal causality / propagation failure:** causal weighting (B6); R3 (Daw et al. 2023, C3).
- **Operator / Jacobian conditioning:**
  - De Ryck et al., ICLR 2024 (D1);
  - Rathore et al., ICML 2024 (A2): loss-Hessian spectrum; Adam + L-BFGS;
  - Liu et al. 2024 (D2; arXiv:2402.00531): condition-number theory and preconditioning;
  - "An analysis and solution of ill-conditioning in PINNs" (arXiv:2405.01957): Jacobian condition (S, search
    only, not yet in the matrix).
- **Optimizer switching** (A2, A7), **adaptive sampling** (C1–C4), **space–time decomposition** (G1–G9) and
  **beam PINNs** (H1–H6).

**Consequences for this analysis:**
- B2-E04 will not claim a mechanism as a discovery.
- At most it can add **benchmark-specific, temporally ordered, paired (modal vs full-field, same init, same
  optimizer switch) evidence**.
- The report's literature section (§8) must state whether the mechanism found is already explained in the literature.
