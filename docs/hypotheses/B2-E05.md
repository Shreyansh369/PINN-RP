# B2-E05 — PRE-REGISTRATION: does window length set the PINN's artificial damping (damping-bias law)?

**Written before any B2-E05 training.** This file is frozen once the first B2-E05 run starts. Later changes are
appended as dated amendments with reasons, and nothing above them is edited.

**Type:** controlled single-axis sweep of the time-window length T_w on the frozen full-field B1 benchmark
(FE-D-M1, fixed-fixed, mode 1, T = 1 s). **No component is novel:** time-marching with hard constraints and
transfer between segments is published (AT-PINN, Chen, Lai, Yang, Thin-Walled Struct. 196, 2024; AT-PINN-HC,
Chen et al., CMAME 436, 2025; `docs/NOVELTY_GAP.md` §E). What is tested is a quantitative **law** relating
T_w to the excess decay, and whether it can be predicted from a reference-free measurement.

## 2. Model under test (brief §0, Q1)

A damped mode u = e^{−(ζ+d)t} cos(w t) of the operator ∂_tt + 2ζ ∂_t + ω₀² has, per unit amplitude, the residual
energy (d² + Δ)² + 4w²d² + E. Here:
- Δ = ω_d² − w² is the frequency mismatch;
- E is the unresolved residual that does not depend on d.

Integrating over one window of length T_w, starting at unit amplitude, gives the window loss

    J(d; T_w) = [ (d² + Δ)² + 4 w² d² + E ] · (1 − exp(−2 (ζ + d) T_w)) / (2 (ζ + d)),     d* = argmin_d J.

- **Long windows:** ζ+d* = sqrt(ζ² + E/(4w²)).
- **Short windows:** d* ≈ E·T_w/(8w²).

Both limits are the ones in the brief when E = R².

## 3. Hypotheses (from the brief §2, verbatim in content)

- **H-E05a.** The excess decay d = fit_lam − 3.54 decreases monotonically as T_w decreases over
  (1.0, 0.25, 0.10, 0.05) s.
- **H-E05b.** With R measured per window from the PINN field and its residual only, the predicted d*(T_w)
  (formula §2, operational definitions §6) matches the measured d within a factor of 2 for T_w ≤ 0.1 s.
- **H-E05c.** At an equal total budget (640k PDE evaluations), the best T_w beats B1 on decay error AND
  persistence AND L2 in 3/3 seeds.
- **H-E05d (cost).** Wall-clock per PDE evaluation stays within 1.5× of B1.

**Falsifiers.** Q1 is rejected for this problem, and Paper 1 falls back to the diagnostic manuscript, if either:
- d does not decrease with T_w; or
- the measured d differs from the prediction by more than 5×.

### Window ansatz (brief §3)

In window k, with local time τ = t − t_k ∈ [0, T_w]:

    u_k(x, τ) = a_k(x) + τ b_k(x) + tanh²(ω₁ τ) Φ(x) A0 N_k(x, τ),     Φ = 16 x² (L − x)² / L⁴

- **Window 0:** a_0 = u0 (the exact IC, exactly as in B1) and b_0 = 0. Window 0 is beampinn's unchanged
  `HardConstrainedFF`. W1000 is therefore the B1 model, and the B1 training path, verbatim.
- **Windows k ≥ 1:** a_k and b_k are the 8-mode projections (§5) of u_{k−1}(·, T_w) and ∂_t u_{k−1}(·, T_w).
- **Mode shapes:** the first 8 analytic fixed-fixed shapes, in the cancellation-free form of B2-E04 Amendment 1.
  They have exact 4th x-derivatives and satisfy u = u_x = 0 at both ends.
- **Exact by construction, for any N_k:** the IC (u_k(x,0) = a_k, ∂_τ u_k(x,0) = b_k) and all four BCs.

### What changes between windows

- **Network.** N_k has B1's architecture, with its time input normalised over [0, T_w] (as in AT-PINN).
  N_k is initialised from N_{k−1} (transfer). Window 0 starts from B1's initial weights (identical draws).
- **Optimiser.** A fresh Adam and a restarted exp-decay schedule in every window (AT-PINN's "reactivating"
  optimiser).
- **Collocation sampler.** One sampler generator (seed + 7, as in B1) runs across all windows. Points are drawn
  uniformly in [0, L] × [0, T_w].
- **Collocation density.** The number of points per epoch is preserved (640). The density per unit time is NOT
  preserved: it is 1/T_w times B1's. Both are logged. A density-preserving arm is outside Blocks 1–3.

## 5. Hand-off and continuity (what is exact and what is not)

**The projection.** At t_k:
- F = u_{k−1}(x, T_w) and G = ∂_t u_{k−1}(x, T_w) are evaluated by autograd on 64 Gauss–Legendre nodes in
  [0, L].
- They are projected in float64 onto the 8 modes: a = M⁻¹ Φᵀ W F, with diagonal M (orthogonal modes).

**Consequences:**
- u and u_t are **exactly** continuous with the *projected* end state of the previous window (unit test).
- The jump between the raw u_{k−1}(·, T_w) and u_k(·, 0) equals the projection remainder. That remainder is
  logged at every hand-off as:
  - `proj_err_u = ‖F − P₈F‖ / ‖F‖` and `proj_err_ut = ‖G − P₈G‖ / ‖G‖` (GL-weighted);
  - the max-abs jumps on a 201-point uniform grid, normalised by A0 and ω·A0.
- **Reported column:** `handoff_projection_error_max` = max over hand-offs of max(proj_err_u, proj_err_ut).

**Declared confound.** Energy in modes above 8 is discarded at every hand-off. This is an excess-decay mechanism
that the §2 law does not contain. It is reported with the results, and is not removed post hoc.

## 6. Measurements (every run; operational definitions fixed now)

**Evaluation of the stitched field.** The trained windows are stitched into one field u(x, t):
- each t belongs to window k = ⌊t / T_w⌋, with t = T in the last window;
- the evaluation is beampinn `evaluate_full` (L2, PDE/IC/BC errors, damped-cosine fit at mid-span) together
  with the frozen `physref.persistence`, all unchanged.

**Amplitude ratio R(t)** at t = 0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and 1.0 s:
- the frozen persistence ratio A_pred(t)/A_exact(t), interpolated linearly on its 4001-point trace;
- near t = 1.0 s the one-period sliding window is truncated at the boundary, exactly as in the frozen metric.

**Measured unresolved residual per window, R_k [s⁻²]:**
- grid: 51 uniform x in [0, L] × 51 uniform τ in [0, T_w], endpoints included;
- computed on the trained window k only, in float32 with autograd;
- R_k = rms(c2 u_xxxx + u_tt + γ u_t) / rms(u);
- no reference solution is used.

**Per-window prediction.**
- d*_k = argmin over d ∈ (−ζ, 200] of J(d; T_w).
- Primary inputs: E = R_k², Δ = 0, w = ω_d = 129.3246 rad/s and ζ = γ/2 = 3.54 1/s. Both constants come
  from the PDE coefficients and the BC eigenproblem (no reference solution).
- Minimiser: scipy `minimize_scalar`, bounded, xatol 1e-10.

**Run prediction.** `predicted_d*` = mean_k d*_k. The log-envelope slope over [0, T] is the time-average of
the per-window excess decays.

**Measured excess decay.** d = fit_lam − 3.54, where fit_lam comes from the frozen damped-cosine fit on 2001
mid-span points over [0, 1] s.

**Sensitivity variants** (reported, NOT used for any decision):
- (i) E = max(R_k² − 4w²d², 0), i.e. the part of R not explained by the measured d;
- (ii) median instead of mean over windows.

**Cost.**
- `train_seconds`: training steps only, with beampinn accounting.
- `handoff_seconds`: projections plus R_k measurement, timed apart.
- H-E05d uses (train_seconds + handoff_seconds) / pde_evaluations, divided by the same quantity for W1000 on
  THIS machine. Runs execute 3 at a time on 4 cores, 1 thread each; the concurrency is declared.

**CSV.** One row per run with the brief §5 columns: arm, seed, T_w, N_windows, L2_exact, L2_late_exact,
persistence_cycles, persistence_cycles_vel, amp_ratio_t0.1/0.25/0.5/0.75/1.0, fit_w, fit_lam, decay_error,
frequency_error, phase_error, PDE_residual_rel, R_k (mean, max, full list), predicted_d*, train_seconds,
pde_evaluations, peak_rss_mb, parameters, handoff_projection_error_max.

## 8. Decision gate after Block 3 (computed in code; seed 1234)

**Inputs.** Let d(T) be the measured excess decay of each arm, and ρ(T) = predicted_d*(T) / d(T) for
T ∈ {0.10, 0.05}. If d(T) ≤ 0, ρ is undefined and counts as outside every band.

**Monotonicity (H-E05a).** H-E05a holds iff d(1.0) > d(0.25) > d(0.10) > d(0.05), strictly. Spearman ρ_s is
reported as context.

**Classification:**

| Label | Condition |
|---|---|
| **PASS** | H-E05a holds AND ρ ∈ [0.5, 2] for both T_w = 0.10 and 0.05 → Paper 1 = law + rule + 3-seed validation |
| **PARTIAL** | H-E05a holds AND the worst ρ is outside [0.5, 2] but inside [0.2, 5] → empirical law, model as qualitative explanation |
| **FAIL (falsifier 1)** | H-E05a does not hold (no monotone trend) → Paper 1 = diagnostic manuscript + homogeneity mechanism section |
| **FAIL (falsifier 2)** | H-E05a holds but some ρ is outside [0.2, 5] → Q1 rejected for this problem |

**Not decided at this gate:**
- H-E05c needs seeds 1235 and 1236 (Block 4; not requested yet).
- H-E05d is reported at the gate but does not change the label.
